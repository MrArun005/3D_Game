# The "GTA slice" brief, mapped onto Halstead Bay — 2026-09-11

Arun pasted a widely shared prompt for an AI-built browser driving game
("a small open-city GTA slice": Three.js + Vite, gripping physics, a camera
that banks with speed, dense wet night streets, a red mid-engine hero car
modelled in Blender, AI traffic, free roam plus a timed route, HUD with
speed / distance / minimap, the game as the first screen, 60 fps). This
document is that brief turned into acceptance checks against this repo, with
what already existed, what landed today, and what is out of reach here.

The brief's own closing advice is the useful part: the result is a loop
(reference, assets, assemble, play-test, critic pass), not a sentence. This
repo has been running that loop since August; the review of 2026-09-09 and
its fix pass are the critic step.

## Not done, and why

- **Blender-modelled cars.** No Blender binary in this environment. The
  pipeline for it exists (`tools/genchar:blender`, `docs/PIPELINE.md`); the
  hero body is still the CC0 Quaternius coupe. A red mid-engine body from
  Blender is an Arun-side task or a session with Blender installed.
- **TypeScript.** The repo is plain ES modules with dense comments and a
  node test runner; converting 29k lines for the brief's sake buys nothing.
- **Rapier.** The brief suggests it for "better physics". This project's
  hand-written tyre model is the better fit for the driving feel and is now
  regression-tested; Rapier would be for ragdolls, not the car.
- **Unity / Godot / Unreal.** Out of scope: this is the browser runtime.

## Play-test checklist (unverified visually)

1. Load: the city is visible immediately behind a low card; after 20 s the
   camera orbits the red car. Clicking ALL CONTROLS expands the list without
   starting the game; clicking anywhere else starts it.
2. The hero is red. Dispatch names it "a red vehicle" at one star.
3. Drive a fast sweeping bend: the camera leans into the turn a few degrees;
   at a crawl it does not lean at all. If it leans the wrong way, flip the
   sign of `bank` in `camera.js` and say so.
4. TRIP under GEAR climbs by 0.1 km per 100 m.
5. Phone → SERVICES → CHECKPOINT RUN → START: eight rings, timer, best time
   shown on the card afterwards; START becomes ABANDON while running.
