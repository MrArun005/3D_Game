# State of the game — physics & aesthetics review, 2026-08-31

Written after a day of hands-on work: post stack (GTAO + bloom) shipped,
placement rules fixed district-wide, avatars wired, street furniture made
destructible. Everything below is grounded in either a screenshot taken today,
a measured number, or a file:line — no vibes. Ratings are against the
project's own bar ("GTA IV-era geometry under modern lighting", CLAUDE.md).

## Verdict in one paragraph

The bones are excellent: a hand-planned district with real road classes, a
tyre model good enough to carry the whole game, a disciplined draw-call
budget, and (since today) a modern post stack and props that break. What
stands between this and its own stated bar is not one big thing — it is
chunk-build hitches you can feel through the wheel, aliasing that crawls on
every thin edge, traffic vehicles that visibly hover, and streets that are
crisp and clean where a lived-in city is worn and wet.

## 2. Car quality (assets)

- **Hero car**: lofted hull with real UVs, library normal/ORM maps, working
  damage (crumple at contact point, deflating tyres, milky glass, fire). Good
  bones. Gaps: no interior, no detachable parts (doors/bumpers/hood as
  sub-meshes is the GTA tell), one silhouette as the hero.
- **Traffic/parked**: six silhouettes ship, but bodies are merged blobs with
  tyres baked in — they visibly hover (ROADMAP 3.4's own words). The rebuild
  (separate spinning wheels, 4,000-tri budget, LODs) is scoped and waiting.
- **Handling classes**: one handling profile exists. `vehicle/config.js`
  BODY_TYPES is the natural hook for per-class mass/torque/tyre profiles
  (muscle / super / van / truck) — mostly data, not code.

## 3. Asset design (the 91-piece kit)

- The kit is chamfered blockouts — correct silhouettes, no baked detail. The
  Blender refinement loop (tools/genchar-style, `.cage.json` per asset) exists
  but hasn't been run over the top-ten most-seen props (ROADMAP 3.1b).
- Facade materials read well at 50 m, flat at 5 m. **Interior mapping
  (Tier 2.2) remains the highest payoff-per-effort item on the whole list** —
  the city is boxes with painted windows, and parallax rooms fix that without
  adding a single draw call.
- Nine parametric characters + two RPM avatar wardrobes are staged and (as of
  today) the avatars drive the player character path with retargeted clips.

## 4. Map quality

Strong: road-class hierarchy reads (arterial vs lane), crosswalks + manholes
+ drains exist, harbour/parks/bridges give districts identity, mountain ring
sells scale. Weak: block interiors repeat (same yard kit), kerbs and pavement
are one uniform colour city-wide, no kerb ramps at crossings, freeway
undersides are bare, and everything is *clean* — no decals, stains, patches
(Tier 2.3 decal system is the fix).

## 5. Night visibility ("night vision")

Atmospheric but genuinely too dark away from lit facades — the spawn shot at
night is silhouettes on a horizon glow. Levers, cheap → real:
1. Nudge night exposure (ACES `toneMappingExposure` 1.0 → ~1.15 at night) and
   hemisphere ground term — one-line experiments, screenshot-comparable.
2. More lamp glow pools on locals (pools are near-free instanced quads).
3. Bloom (shipped today) already lifts lit windows.
4. The real fix is Tier 1.4 clustered lights: 24–48 true lights around the
   camera so lamps light pavement and shops light the street.

## 6. Roads and markings

Present and correct: lane dashes, double-yellow centrelines, zebra crossings
(traffic actually gates on them), ironwork. Missing for the GTA read: stop
lines at signals, turn arrows in lanes, paint weathering (every line is
factory-fresh white), kerb ramps, and street-name signs with real names —
`places.js` has names; the sign prop exists; they've never been married.

## 7. Auto lamps & lighting quality

- Street lamps ARE automatic (baked emissive + pools at night, dimmed by
  `daylightAssets()` at noon). Car headlights default ON — including in
  daylight, where two real shadowless spotlights burn cost invisibly.
  **Cheap win: default `headlights = !DAY`.**
- Post stack now: GTAO (denoised) + emissive-fed bloom + grade, single
  pipeline, measured +13 draw calls. Next in the roadmap's own order:
  TAA/SMAA → CSM shadows (the near/far two-sun hack is decent but kerbs 5 m
  away still soft) → **SSR** (three ships `SSRNode`; the pipeline already has
  depth+normals; wet tarmac reflecting taillights is the single money shot)
  → clustered lights → volumetrics (`GodraysNode` ships too).

## 8. Smooth driving experience

- The tyre/suspension model is the best thing in the project (20 unit tests,
  weight transfer, per-surface grip). What breaks the feel:
  1. **Chunk hitches** (see Glitch #1) — a 60 ms frame mid-corner is felt as
     a steering glitch even when fps recovers instantly.
  2. **Steering authority at speed** — full-lock response at 90 km/h spins
     the car (reproduced repeatedly under automation today). GTA scales
     steering angle down with speed; `stepVehicle` clamps rate but not
     speed-scaled angle. Worth a tuning pass with a hand on the wheel.
  3. Kerb mounting is well-handled (smoothstepped ramp in `metrics.js`).

## 9. Research notes — free capabilities already in the engine

three r185 ships, as TSL nodes we can wire into the existing pipeline at zero
licence cost: `SSRNode`, `TRAANode`/`SMAANode`/`FXAANode`, `SSGINode` (GI!),
`GodraysNode`, `DepthOfFieldNode`, `MotionBlur`, `LensflareNode`,
`ChromaticAberrationNode`. Physics: the debris integrator shipped today is
deliberately hand-rolled (repo style); if articulated ragdolls become a goal,
Rapier (MIT, wasm) is the free option — but crowd figures are 6-part
instanced, so a Verlet 6-body chain in-house may fit better. Audio: radio +
dispatch must be CC0 (repo rule) — Kenney packs / freesound CC0 for barks,
concatenative dispatch from a free TTS (e.g. Piper) rendered offline to clips.

## Proposed order of attack

1. **Tier 1.3 time-sliced chunk builds** — biggest felt improvement per line.
2. **SMAA now, TAA when stable** — kills the crawl everywhere.
3. **SSR on wet roads** — the night-city money shot; pipeline is ready.
4. Small wins batch: day headlights off, night exposure nudge, stop lines +
   turn arrows in the road paint pass.
5. **Traffic vehicle rebuild (3.4)** — stops the hover, adds wheel spin.
6. **Interior mapping (2.2)** — the city stops being boxes.
7. Decals (2.3): road wear, stains, patches.
8. Crowd skinned pool (3.5) — the parametric cast is already staged.
