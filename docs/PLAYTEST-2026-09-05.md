# Play-test checklist — 2026-09-05 evening build

Everything below landed today without a browser check (no Playwright in this
project). Each line is one thing to look at; a wrong one is a one-line fix in
the file named. Live: https://3d-game.arunmarun005.workers.dev (Cloudflare
deploys every push to main; hard-reload if a string below is missing).

## Shooting layer

- **Tracers** (`game/tracers.js`): every police round and yours is a short
  bright streak moving along the ray, blooming. If they are invisible, the
  `glow(m, 1.6)` strength in tracers.js is the knob; if they smear the AO
  (dark speckles behind them), the mrt guard is not applying.
- **Brass** (`game/weapon.js:eject`): a casing pops right and up out of the
  breech on every shot, bounces once, lies there. If it comes out of the
  wrong side, flip the sign of `rx, rz` in `eject()`.
- **Muzzle wisp + officers' muzzles** (`world/puffs.js`): a small grey puff
  at each muzzle. Officers' muzzles also light the street briefly
  (`traffic.flashLight`, borrowed from your weapon's point light).
- **Near-miss whiz** (`audio.whiz`): a round going past pans to the side it
  came from and nudges the camera a hair. If mirrored, flip the sign in
  main.js `onShot` where it says so.
- **Hits on you**: blood spray off you on foot, sparks off the bodywork in
  the car (`weapon.sparksAt`), the red **hurt vignette** at the frame edge
  (`grade.setHurt`) pulses per hit and stays lit under 40% health.
- **Headshots**: aim at an officer's head — `HEADSHOT` flashes, 3x damage.
- **Blood pools** under downed officers and pedestrians (`DecalPool` with
  colour/radius). Downed officers sink into the road over their last 1.5 s.
- **Pickups**: dropped guns/grenades/vests turn slowly, have a breathing
  glow disc (white / green / blue), blink for their last six seconds.
- **Bullets break street furniture**: shoot a bin, cone or meter.
- **Parked-car alarm**: shoot a parked car — a two-tone chirp for five seconds.

## Police

- **Patrol**: at zero stars one cruiser drives around with lights off. Every
  so often it "answers a call": lights + siren for eight seconds, speeds past.
- **Witnesses**: shoot into the air in an empty alley — nothing. Do it near
  pedestrians or a cruiser — stars.
- **Siren** follows the nearest hunting cruiser (louder as it closes, panned).
- **Light pool**: hunting cruisers throw a red/blue wash on the tarmac.
- **Drive-bys**: at 3★ a cruiser with a line on you inside 42 m fires from
  the passenger side.
- **Tyres**: at 3★ one landed round in eight on the car takes a tyre
  (`TYRE SHOT OUT`); the car pulls and drags on it. The spike strip now
  actually works (`SPIKED · TYRES GONE`). The garage repairs both.
- **Disabling vehicles**: eight rounds into a car or cruiser stop its engine
  (`CRUISER DISABLED` / `ENGINE OUT`), it smokes from the bonnet, a
  civilian's driver bails and runs, a cruiser's officers step out.
- **Evasion**: break line of sight and stay unseen ~10 s (20 s at 4★+): the
  minimap shows a growing dashed search ring where they last saw you,
  cruisers sweep it slowly, dispatch says "sweeping the block", the
  helicopter orbits the last-seen point, then `YOU LOST THEM`. Get seen
  again: "Visual regained!".
- **Officers walk** between covers now (no 8 m teleport); when they have
  lost you they face where they last had you.
- **New star**: `WANTED LEVEL n` flashes.

## Cars and city

- **Crashes** spark and raise dust at the contact point above a moderate hit.
- **Tyre smoke** behind the rear wheels when the back end slides.
- **Stuck drivers honk** at whatever blocks them — park across a lane.
- **Grenades**: a fireball that swells and fades, ten dark smoke puffs, a
  ~2 m scorch on the road, vehicles inside the blast shoved and damaged
  (no car fireballs, by your rule).
- **AO speckles at lamp cones / searchlight / puddles / billboards** should
  be gone: every additive material now carries the MRT normal guard
  (`core/additive.js`).

## Robustness

- A frame-loop throw no longer blacks the screen: the last good frame keeps
  drawing and `FRAME ERROR · …` flashes on the HUD. If you see that, the
  message is the bug.

## Frame rate

Nothing here adds more than one draw per pool (tracers, puffs, casings,
blood decals, halos per drop, one pool disc per cruiser). Please read the
F3 overlay at kingsway-corner and in a 3★ firefight and tell me the two
numbers; I have not measured today's work.
