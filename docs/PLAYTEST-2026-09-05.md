# Play-test checklist — 2026-09-05 evening build

Everything below landed today without a browser check (no Playwright in this
project). Each line is one thing to look at; a wrong one is a one-line fix in
the file named. Live: https://3d-game.arunmarun005.workers.dev (Cloudflare
deploys every push to main; hard-reload if a string below is missing).

## Shooting layer

- **Tracers** (`game/tracers.js`): every police round and yours is a short
  bright streak moving along the ray, blooming. If they are invisible, the
  `glow(m, 1.6)` strength in tracers.js is the knob; if they smear the AO
  (dark speckles behind them), the mrt guard is not applying.
- **Brass** (`game/weapon.js:eject`): a casing pops right and up out of the
  breech on every shot, bounces once, lies there. If it comes out of the
  wrong side, flip the sign of `rx, rz` in `eject()`.
- **Muzzle wisp + officers' muzzles** (`world/puffs.js`): a small grey puff
  at each muzzle. Officers' muzzles also light the street briefly
  (`traffic.flashLight`, borrowed from your weapon's point light).
- **Near-miss whiz** (`audio.whiz`): a round going past pans to the side it
  came from and nudges the camera a hair. If mirrored, flip the sign in
  main.js `onShot` where it says so.
- **Hits on you**: blood spray off you on foot, sparks off the bodywork in
  the car (`weapon.sparksAt`), the red **hurt vignette** at the frame edge
  (`grade.setHurt`) pulses per hit and stays lit under 40% health.
- **Headshots**: aim at an officer's head — `HEADSHOT` flashes, 3x damage.
- **Blood pools** under downed officers and pedestrians (`DecalPool` with
  colour/radius). Downed officers sink into the road over their last 1.5 s.
- **Pickups**: dropped guns/grenades/vests turn slowly, have a breathing
  glow disc (white / green / blue), blink for their last six seconds.
- **Bullets break street furniture**: shoot a bin, cone or meter.
- **Parked-car alarm**: shoot a parked car — a two-tone chirp for five seconds.

## Police

- **Patrol**: at zero stars one cruiser drives around with lights off. Every
  so often it "answers a call": lights + siren for eight seconds, speeds past.
- **Witnesses**: shoot into the air in an empty alley — nothing. Do it near
  pedestrians or a cruiser — stars.
- **Siren** follows the nearest hunting cruiser (louder as it closes, panned).
- **Light pool**: hunting cruisers throw a red/blue wash on the tarmac.
- **Drive-bys**: at 3★ a cruiser with a line on you inside 42 m fires from
  the passenger side.
- **Tyres**: at 3★ one landed round in eight on the car takes a tyre
  (`TYRE SHOT OUT`); the car pulls and drags on it. The spike strip now
  actually works (`SPIKED · TYRES GONE`). The garage repairs both.
- **Disabling vehicles**: eight rounds into a car or cruiser stop its engine
  (`CRUISER DISABLED` / `ENGINE OUT`), it smokes from the bonnet, a
  civilian's driver bails and runs, a cruiser's officers step out.
- **Evasion**: break line of sight and stay unseen ~10 s (20 s at 4★+): the
  minimap shows a growing dashed search ring where they last saw you,
  cruisers sweep it slowly, dispatch says "sweeping the block", the
  helicopter orbits the last-seen point, then `YOU LOST THEM`. Get seen
  again: "Visual regained!".
- **Officers walk** between covers now (no 8 m teleport); when they have
  lost you they face where they last had you.
- **New star**: `WANTED LEVEL n` flashes.

## Cars and city

- **Crashes** spark and raise dust at the contact point above a moderate hit.
- **Tyre smoke** behind the rear wheels when the back end slides.
- **Stuck drivers honk** at whatever blocks them — park across a lane.
- **Grenades**: a fireball that swells and fades, ten dark smoke puffs, a
  ~2 m scorch on the road, vehicles inside the blast shoved and damaged
  (no car fireballs, by your rule).
- **AO speckles at lamp cones / searchlight / puddles / billboards** should
  be gone: every additive material now carries the MRT normal guard
  (`core/additive.js`).

## Robustness

- A frame-loop throw no longer blacks the screen: the last good frame keeps
  drawing and `FRAME ERROR · …` flashes on the HUD. If you see that, the
  message is the bug.

## Added after the list above

- Marksmen (4★) now **fall on their roof** and lie there; every shooter
  (officers, rooftops, roadblock posts, the helicopter door gun) shows a
  **muzzle flash** when it fires.
- The nearest hunting cruiser inside 40 m casts a **real red/blue light**
  on the buildings (`lighting.js` beacon); cruisers also get headlights.
- **Hit marker sound**: a tick per body hit, a two-note ding per headshot.
- **1★ officers do not shoot** unless you fired in the last eight seconds;
  they come to cuff you. From 2★ they fire on sight.

- **Storm nights**: lightning every 18-50 s (two or three white stutters on
  the hemisphere light, the rain shows for a moment), thunder 0.4-3 s later
  with a crack when it is close (`world/lightning.js`, `audio.thunder`).

- **NPC pursuits**: two in five of the patrol's "calls" are a real one -- it
  chases a random civilian with lights and siren, pulls it over (both stop,
  lights going, six seconds), then leaves. Nothing to do with you.

- **SWAT**: at 4★ deployed officers, rooftop marksmen and roadblock posts
  wear black helmets with a visor band, plate carriers with a white POLICE
  strip, gloves and knee pads (`officer.js` SWAT set, `dressOfficer`).

- **Vigilante**: when a patrol chase starts within 150 m of you (zero stars),
  `VIGILANTE · STOP THE FLEEING CAR` -- shoot its engine out or pin it until
  it stops, within 16 m, before the patrol does: +$400. Tactical arrival at
  4★ is announced (`TACTICAL UNIT ON SCENE`).

- **Rain breathes** (night): intensity drifts 0.35-1.0 over ~10 min; the
  sheet, its sound and the road grip follow it (up to 28% less grip when
  it pours); lightning only in the heavy spells.

- **Wet tarmac**: the road goes glossier as the rain gets heavier (roughness
  0.48 -> 0.18, reflection up); puddle spray scales with the rain.
- **SWAT grenades** (4★): hide behind a car for four seconds within 10-28 m
  of a tactical officer and he lobs a grenade onto your last-seen spot
  ("Frag out!"). Move.

- **Rain on the normal cycle**: without `?night`, rain now arrives in spells
  during the clock's night (about 40% of night time, ~20 min spells) and
  clears by morning; `?night` still rains all night. Dry = matte road, full
  grip, no lightning.

- **Puddles** appear with the rain and dry out over about three minutes
  after it stops (`PuddleSystem.wetness`); a dry day has none.
- **Rain and the city**: civilians ease off up to 28% below cruise when it
  pours; pedestrians hurry (up to 35% faster).
- **Rainy sky**: the dome darkens with the rain (whites out on lightning),
  fog thickens up to 1.9x, stars vanish under cloud.

## Little Tokyo (blocks around 2280-2560 x 1395-1515, just south-east of spawn)

- Every building there is now generated by `world/tokyo.js` -- no kit, no
  Kenney: tiled facades in eight palettes, storey bands, lit/unlit windows
  (warm/cool), balconies and air-con units on the backs and sides, a
  shopfront glass band with an awning, a fascia sign, vertical kanban sign
  columns on the street corners carrying the atlas's Japanese tiles, neon
  tube lines along the floors, parapets, water tanks, antennas, stair
  bulkheads and rooftop billboards. One mesh per chunk. At night the
  windows, neon and kanban glow and bloom; by day they are dark.
- The street too: concrete utility poles every ~22 m along the kerbs with
  cross-arms, two sagging power lines per span and a drop to the buildings
  (one LineSegments per chunk), red paper lanterns under a third of the
  awnings, and kit vending machines / A-frames / stalls in front of the
  buildings. Wires at 1 px may read thin on a 1440 screen; that is the
  WebGPU line width limit, not a bug.
- The two 'tower' blocks carry a few 18-24 storey landmark slabs with wide
  neon-framed rooftop billboards; the park block has a shrine (red torii,
  lit stone lanterns, a small hall).
- Crowds: while you are in Little Tokyo the pedestrian fleet concentrates on
  its pavements (six in ten spawns elsewhere are thrown back) and more of
  them stand -- it should read noticeably busier than Kingsway next door.
- Four-way junctions in the district are scramble crossings (two diagonal
  zebra rows through the centre). If the diagonals miss the junction, the
  `len`/`half` factors in districtWorld #signals are the knob.
- About one glowing part in seven (neon strips, lanterns, kanban columns)
  buzzes and stutters at night; if the whole street flickers, the `flick`
  threshold in `tokyoMaterial` is wrong.
- Kanban panels are rolled 90 degrees (the lettering runs up the column,
  2.6 m per tenant). If they read upside down or mirrored, the roll sign in
  districtWorld's `vertical` branch is the fix.
- If a building faces the wrong way (kanban and awning on the alley side),
  the street-side probe in `frontRotation` is the fix. `?notokyo` restores
  the old massing for comparison.

A story mission runs through the district: LITTLE TOKYO: THE YOKOCHO DEBT
(ramen alley -> hold Kabukicho, three down -> lose the heat at the capsule
hotel), $9,000.

Photo mode has a `little-tokyo` preset (P, then `]`, or `window.photo.goto('little-tokyo')`;
Enter prints the stats line) -- please paste that line, it is the district's cost.

- **Time of day persists** across reloads (`hb.clock`); `?night` / `?dusk`
  still pin it. Crossing into a district flashes its name; while wanted,
  dispatch calls out where you are heading.

- **Idle camera**: park and touch nothing for 20 s and the camera drifts
  into a slow orbit of the car; any input ends it.
- **Horn (I)** in the car: pedestrians ahead scatter, the car in front picks
  up for three seconds.

- **The street talks back**: honk at pedestrians or raise a gun near them and
  one of them shouts (light quick voice, STREET channel, at most one per 6 s).
- **Dispatch speaks**: radio lines are read by the browser's speech synthesis
  (fast, low, quiet). If the voice grates, `?novoice`; if it talks over the
  game, the volume/rate in `chatter.js #speak` are the knobs.
